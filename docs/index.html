Pattern modifications

When crafting with seed beads, I often work with non-beading patterns. It can be a friendship bracelet pattern from Bracelet Book or retro pixel art. These designs usually require a few tweaks: adding or trimming rows, adjusting proportions to fit the seed beads aspect ratio, picking the bead colors, and so forth.

I compile these modifications into PDF files and share them just like the originals: non-commercial and completely free. These mods are created with a community-driven mindset, much like open-source software.

Design authors are credited.

Fields of Shimmering Blooms

  • Widened to 13 beads to fit a standard clasp tube.
  • Added 8 rows to extend the length to approximately 17cm.
  • Made minor changes to the petal design to match modifications.
  • Stretched vertically by 1.25x to adjust for the bead aspect ratio.

Silent Moon Mariners

  • Stretched vertically by 1.25x to adjust for the bead aspect ratio.
  • The moon was redrawn to maintain its 1:1 circular ratio after the stretch.
  • The bubble color was replaced with white (looks much better in beads).
  • Three length options: conventionally 16cm, 15cm, and 13cm.

Wristband of Eerie Night

  • Stretched vertically by 1.25x to adjust for the bead aspect ratio.
  • Added side rows to space ghosts away from the clasps.
  • Three length options: conventionally men's, women's, and kids' sizes.
