Pattern mods

Crafting something with seed beads I often work with non-beading patterns. It can be a friendship bracelet from the Bracelet Book or a pixel art sprite from a video game. Either way it needs to be modified for beading. Usually it means adding or cutting few lines, removing some details, finding beads colors and so on. I save these modifications in the PDF files and share them same as the original patterns were. Everything is free. Design authors are credited.

Fields of Shimmering Blooms

  • Widened to 13 beads to fit a standard clasp tube.
  • Added 8 rows to extend the length to approximately 17cm.
  • Made minor changes to the petal design to match modifications.
  • Stretched vertically by 1.25x to adjust for the bead aspect ratio.
